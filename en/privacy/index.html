Privacy

Last updated 11.10.2026 · Norsk · Polski

In short

Cosmo Shield has no accounts, no ads and no profiling, and we sell no data. The game sets no cookies.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Jacek Mariusz Taczała (a private individual, the author of the game), contact: personvern@stop60.no.

Cookie-free statistics

We count visits with GoatCounter (the script is hosted with the game): page and referring page, browser and system, language, screen width, country (derived from the IP address) and the events “game started”, “wave repelled” and “game over” (with the wave number) and “game shared”. The IP address and browser are only used in the server's memory (up to 8 hours) to count unique visits; only aggregate statistics are stored, without IP address or identifier.

Purpose: to know how many people play and where they come from. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object, e.g. by blocking scripts.

Data on your device

The browser's storage (localStorage) keeps your language, sound and auto-fire settings, a game in progress (so you can continue) and your own statistics (best score, best wave, games played). These are not cookies and never leave the device. You can delete them in your browser's settings for this site.

Recipients

GitHub, Inc. (USA) – hosting via GitHub Pages; GitHub may log the IP address and browser data when pages are loaded.

E-mails to stop60.no addresses are forwarded by Domeneshop AS (Norway) to Proton AG (Switzerland) and only used to reply.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object. Write to personvern@stop60.no. You can also complain to Datatilsynet (Norway) or UODO (Poland).
